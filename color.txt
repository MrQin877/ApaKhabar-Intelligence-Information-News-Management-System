USAGE
-----
Primary actions          brand-600 / brand-500 dark
Hover actions            brand-700 / brand-400 dark
Positive states          Success roles
General information      Info roles
Pending or attention     Warning roles
Critical or emergency    Danger roles
Borders and surfaces     Greyscale palette

Border radius             0.75rem
Large radius              var(--radius)
Medium radius             calc(var(--radius) - 2px)
Small radius              calc(var(--radius) - 4px)

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
-------------------
The current .dark theme references --brand-950 for success-bg, but
brand-950 is not defined. Add a brand-950 token or use a defined dark
success background before relying on dark success alerts.
