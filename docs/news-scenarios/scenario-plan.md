## Natural comment examples

- Road: “Kalau berangkat kerja pukul tujuh, apakah motor masih bisa lewat?”
- Health: “Apakah orang tua yang tidak punya ponsel bisa mendaftar langsung?”
- Community cleaning: “Saya bisa ikut setelah pukul delapan. Masih boleh menyusul?”
- Workshop: “Kalau belum punya usaha, tetapi ingin belajar, apakah boleh ikut?”
- Library: “Apakah buku cerita anak bisa dipinjam untuk dibaca di rumah?”
- Waste collection: “Untuk gang di belakang lapangan, jadwalnya ikut wilayah mana?”
- Market: “Pendaftaran stan masih dibuka untuk penjual makanan rumahan?”
- Meeting: “Mohon penerangan di dekat lapangan juga masuk pembahasan.”

## Later analytics work

Agree a date range first. Spread publication across several weeks, with varied engagement. Generate interaction records first and derive counters consistently. Total views must be at least unique viewers; likes must match distinct user/post records. Interactions must occur after publication and account creation, never in the future. Separate published, pending, and hidden comments. Record moderation history only for actions represented in the scenario.

Keep genuine social snapshots and connections intact. Do not fabricate Meta API results or present synthetic engagement as measured activity. Review existing calculations with consistent replacement data before changing dashboard logic or layout.
